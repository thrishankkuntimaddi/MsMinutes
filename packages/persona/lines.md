# Her lines

Scripted lines she says word for word when she hears a specific command, instead of making up a reply.

- Each line starts with `## when:` and the commands that trigger it, separated by `|`. Matching ignores capitals, punctuation and "Miss"/"Ms. Minutes", and forgives a small speech-recognition slip, but the command has to be most of what you said.
- Below it, exactly what she says. Commas and `…` become her pauses; tags like `[playful 0.6]` or `[jump]` change her face and move her, just like in her normal replies.

Edit freely and restart the brain.
